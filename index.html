Who We Are

Empowering the Aerospace Ecosystem

EMMA Aerospace Consulting LLP is a Canadian-registered limited liability partnership delivering world-class advisory services at the intersection of aerospace innovation, policy, and technology.

Our Mission

To bridge sovereign, institutional, and commercial stakeholders within the global aerospace ecosystem by delivering program management, compliance, and industrial participation frameworks aligned with international standards.

Global Presence

Operating across Europe, Central Asia, and the Middle East, we facilitate integration into global aerospace supply chains and foster strategic partnerships across continents.

Our Approach

Combining deep technical expertise with strategic vision, we deliver tailored solutions that align with ESA ECSS, EASA, ISO, and NASA specifications.
